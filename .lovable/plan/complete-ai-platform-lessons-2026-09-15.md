# Complete AI Platform Lessons

## Goal
Give every AI platform shown in Guides its own complete learning pathway, with no missing “Learn” option or unrelated fallback lessons.

## What will change
- Add full, platform-specific courses for Midjourney, DALL·E, Notion AI, Canva AI, Copy.ai, Perplexity AI, and AI for Business.
- Match each guide card’s advertised lesson count and organize lessons into clear beginner-to-practical modules.
- Include actionable explanations, prompt examples, exercises, responsible-use guidance, and a final applied project appropriate to each platform.
- Keep the existing Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Jasper AI, and Stable Diffusion courses intact.
- Make every guide card show both **Learn** and **Try AI**, using one shared course catalog for counts and navigation.
- Keep progress tracking, sequential unlocking, and certificates working with each platform’s existing slug.

## Technical details
- Store the added course content in a focused data module and merge it into the existing course catalog.
- Derive guide lesson counts and availability from the course catalog to prevent future mismatches.
- Validate that every displayed guide slug resolves to a course and every declared lesson total matches its modules.
- Check lesson pathways and lesson pages on desktop and mobile, then confirm the preview has no errors.
