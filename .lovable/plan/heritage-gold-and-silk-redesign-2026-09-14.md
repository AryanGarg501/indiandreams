# Heritage gold and silk redesign

## Goal
Refresh Indian Dreams with the selected cinematic heritage direction and tasteful animated background scenes across the whole website, without changing its content or learning flows.

## Visual direction
- Keep the established emerald, ivory, and gold identity while adding deeper silk-like contrast and editorial hierarchy.
- Introduce a reusable animated scene made from flowing silk bands, fine heritage geometry, luminous learning paths, and subtle gold particles—no generic neon effects or decorative blobs.
- Preserve the existing learner photography, logo, pricing, page copy, and actions.

## Implementation
- Build one lightweight background-scene component with public, account/payment, and dashboard variants.
- Add the cinematic scene to the homepage first viewport, public pages, sign-in and checkout journeys, dashboard pages, certificate views, and mini-games through shared route-aware placement.
- Restyle the homepage first viewport to closely match the chosen centered, cinematic composition while keeping all existing proof points and actions.
- Refine global typography, surfaces, borders, shadows, and entrance animations so the remaining sections feel cohesive rather than individually rebuilt.
- Respect reduced-motion settings, keep animation layers non-interactive, and reduce effects on small screens for performance.

## Verification
- Check key public, account, payment, dashboard, and mobile views for readability, clipping, navigation, and animation layering.
- Confirm the app compiles cleanly and has no new runtime errors.
